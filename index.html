Questions people ask.

Is BioMed Expo free?

The expo floor is free and the conference is paid. Alien Event tickets include BioMed lectures and the full exhibit hall.

Can I buy a single day?

Yes. BioMed Expo sells day passes at $120 per day.

How old do I need to be?

You need to be 18 or older to attend.

Where do I park?

Parking is free at the resort and at the Virgin Casino across the street.

Can I get a refund?

Refund rules are set per Eventbrite listing. Read the policy on the page you buy from.

Can I watch from home?

Yes. The live stream costs $99 for all three days and shows four channels at once.
